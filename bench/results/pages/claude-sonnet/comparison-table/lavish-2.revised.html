Tradeoff to weigh: Pinecone is fastest and needs no ops but costs the most ($3,100) and is proprietary. Qdrant is the cheapest option that meets the target ($1,250). pgvector is the cheapest overall (about $400) and reuses our Postgres, but at 95 ms it misses the 50 ms target. Milvus's cost excludes ops time, which is not quantified here.
