Migration plan · needs your decision

Move user sessions from Redis to Postgres

Approve the plan and pick a rollout strategy at the bottom of the page.

Why now

Sessions live only in Redis and are lost on failover. Last month that forced 38,214 users to log in again.

Goal: durable sessions, no forced logout, auth p99 at or under 45 ms.

Request path after the change

Redis stays as a read-through cache in front of Postgres. Sessions are stored durably in a new sessions table.

Phases

Durations are proportional in the bar below. Run back to back they add up to 17 days (1 week + 2 days + 1 week + 1 day).

  1. 1 · Table and dual-write

    1 week

    Create the sessions table and dual-write.

  2. 2 · Backfill

    2 days

    Backfill existing sessions.

  3. 3 · Switch reads

    1 week

    Switch reads to Postgres behind the sessions_pg flag.

  4. 4 · Remove Redis writes

    1 day

    Stop writing sessions to Redis.
