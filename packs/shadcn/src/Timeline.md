## Use for
Events in time order: a release history, an incident timeline, project milestones, a roadmap of what is done and what is next.

## Not for
- Tasks with durations that overlap: Gantt, when it ships; until then, a Table with start and end columns.
- An ordered method with no dates: Steps.

## Limits
- About 12 items. More: split by quarter or phase, or keep only the milestones.

## Usual errors
- Items out of order. List them oldest first.
- Mixed date formats ("Oct 28", "2026-11-03"). Pick one.
- Every item "current". Only the item in progress is current; the ones after it are upcoming.
