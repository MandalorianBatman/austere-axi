## Use for
A short ordered method where the order is the point: a rollout plan, an onboarding path, a how-it-works explainer.

## Not for
- Events at real dates, where spacing shows elapsed time: Timeline.
- A route that branches on a decision: Flowchart, when it ships; until then, Architecture with the branches as edges.
- Work handed between teams: Swimlane.

## Limits
- 3 to 7 steps. More: group them into phases, one Steps each.
- One title and one or two sentences for each step.

## Usual errors
- Strings for items: write `StepsItem("Title", "details")` for each step.
- Steps for a checklist with no order. Use a MarkDownRenderer list.
