Write it as `ScatterChart([ScatterSeries(name, [Point(x, y), ...])], xLabel, yLabel)`.

## Use for
How two quantities relate across many items: latency against payload size, effort against impact, to find clusters and outliers.

## Not for
- One value for each category: BarComparison (BarChart).
- A value over time: LineTrend (LineChart).
- Items placed by judgment in four named boxes: a Table, or Quadrant.

## Limits
- 5 to 30 points. Fewer: say it in a sentence. More: bin them.
- Up to 3 series.

## Usual errors
- Both axes without labels. Name each axis and its unit.
- A trend claimed where the points are scattered. Say what the points show.
